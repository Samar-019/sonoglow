Afterglow Player — Visual Views

Designed for Presence & Atmosphere

A player built from the ground up for modern monitors and desktop workflows.

Synced Moving Lyrics

Powered by LRCLIB integration, lyric phrases move seamlessly with high-contrast glowing typography. Enjoy reading along in full-screen Cinema view or docked alongside the cover art.

Somewhere down the line, we found our way
Living in the afterglow of yesterday

Ghostery Ad Protection

Double-layer suppression: Ghostery network filtering blocks tracking requests upfront, while our in-stream detection segment mutes and skips ad segments smoothly.

Full-Window Video Canvas

YouTube Music's video stream expands across the entire canvas with custom ambient contrast tuning, blur controls, and glowing backlight highlights.

Dimensional Album Fan & Swiping

Experience intuitive physical interaction. Swipe across the 3D cover fan to effortlessly navigate neighboring tracks, browse your queue, or tap to play.

Local Audio & Custom Wallpapers

Import your personal audio files (.mp3, .flac, .wav) and set custom animated video or picture wallpapers for custom ambiance.

Electron 45 & Native Desktop Integration

Minimalist system tray integration, system media transport controls, single-instance lock, custom window framing, and hardware-accelerated fluid motion.

Effortless Keyboard Controls

Control everything without lifting your hands from the keyboard.
