Designed for pure atmosphere

Every element was handcrafted to turn passive listening into a mesmerizing visual journey.

🎴

Dimensional Album Fan

Layered cover carousel with natural swipe gestures, a tilted Continue Playing queue, and fluid glass transition effects.

✨

Synced Real-Time Lyrics

Lyrics powered by LRCLIB that move smoothly with the song. Customize phrase size, timing offsets, and cinema presentation.

🛡️

Ghostery Ad Protection

Two layers of defense: network filtering before playback starts, plus automated detection, muting, and instant skipping of ad segments.

🌌

Full-Window Video Canvas

YouTube Music plays seamlessly behind translucent floating glass controls, turning your monitor into a dynamic visualizer.

🎵

Local + Online Library

Search millions of YouTube Music songs, save your favorite playlists, or import your personal offline MP3, FLAC, and WAV audio tracks.

🪟

Ambient Desktop Overlays

Floating mini-player, always-on-top mode, ambient desktop clock, blur adjustments, and full keyboard media hotkey integration.
